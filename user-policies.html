1. Who these policies cover

These User policies apply to everyone who uses a Nethub seat, Sunday lab, campus club, Community chair, facilitator role, or the public website: students, mentors, guests, term leaders, and staff. They apply in the room, on Zoom, in chat, and on shared ranges.

2. Conduct

Treat people in the room as colleagues. Harassment, hate, sexualised talk aimed at minors, or intimidation is not tolerated. Do not record another learner without a clear lab reason and the desk’s leave. Facilitators model the same standard.

Everyone is a mentor: share what you just learned; do not gatekeep a terminal.

3. Acceptable use of labs

  • Attack and defend only inside environments Nethub provides, or systems you own, or systems covered by a written client statement of work.
  • Do not use wordlists, exploits, OSINT collection, or hash recovery against other people’s accounts or networks.
  • Do not attempt to bypass seat grants, admin desks, or payment.
  • Do not mine, spam, or host unrelated services on lab hardware.
  • Report range breakage to the desk; do not “test” production Nethub systems except under Responsible disclosure.

4. Your content

Write-ups, exam answers, and chat you post remain yours, but you grant Nethub a licence to host them for the lab. Do not upload malware droppers, other people’s personal data, or copyrighted courseware you do not have rights to share.

5. Community chairs

A sponsored Community chair is the same lab as every other seat. It is not a separate country page and not a settlement label. Misuse of a sponsored month (selling the seat, sharing logins) ends the chair for that period.

6. Enforcement

The desk may warn, pause a module, or close a seat. Criminal misuse may be referred to the relevant authority. Questions: contact page. These policies may be updated with a new date at the top of this page.
