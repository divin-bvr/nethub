Questions

FAQ

Short answers for students, sponsors, and small companies. This is Nethub — not a Cybrary help center.

Do I need a cybersecurity background?

No. Labs are zero-baseline. We start in a live Linux environment, not a slide deck.

Where do you meet?

Weekend sessions at two universities in Uganda and one in the DRC. Every Sunday the whole network meets online. Refugee learners, including in Kyangwali, join the same rooms.

What does it cost?

About $25 a month for paying students, sometimes less. Refugee and underserved seats are funded by sponsors, NGOs, and institutions.

Is this only training?

We train and we apply it. Small companies can hire us for pentesting, awareness, hardening, forensics, network security, web-app security, and WAF deployment.

How do I facilitate?

Advanced trainees become facilitators. See Facilitate.
