Manufacturing Cybersecurity: How to Make Your Organization More Secure

The manufacturing industry is consistently among the most targeted sectors for cybercriminals due to its operational and strategic importance. Ransomware, intellectual property theft, and supply chain attacks are especially prevalent, with attackers exploiting outdated systems, supply chain vulnerabilities, and the need for continuous operations — all of which highlight the urgent need for stronger manufacturing cybersecurity measures.

Multiple Factors Are Making Manufacturers More Vulnerable

Although digital threat actors once focused more on the healthcare and finance industries, today manufacturers face as much as 26 percent of all cyber attacks. Here are some reasons why this segment has become so vulnerable to attacks:
Get a Demo

Legacy Technology

Older legacy infrastructure often means inadequate security, which can leave manufacturers increasingly vulnerable to attacks.

Long Supply Chains

Because manufacturers work with many companies across their supply chain, an attack on one can easily translate into an attack on them.

Interconnected Systems

As more manufacturers introduce IoT devices and blur the lines between IT and OT, they are also expanding their attack surface for potential threats.

Expensive Downtime

Downtime is always expensive, but this is especially the case for manufacturers — a fact that digital threat actors have been eager to exploit.
