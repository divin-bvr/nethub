What we can take on

Offensive

Penetration testing

Scoped tests of web apps, networks, and exposed services. We show what an attacker can do, then sit with you on the fix list.

People

Cybersecurity awareness

Short, practical sessions for staff: phishing, passwords, devices, and what to do when something feels wrong. Built for small teams, not Fortune 100 slideware.

Defense

Security hardening

Baselines for servers, identity, endpoints, and cloud. Close the defaults that stay open because nobody had time.

After an incident

Forensics

Preserve evidence, reconstruct what happened, and give leadership a clear picture — without pretending every shop has a SOC.

Infrastructure

Network security

Segmentation, remote access, wifi, and the boring architecture decisions that stop a laptop compromise from becoming a company-wide event.

Applications

Web app security & WAF

Review the app your customers use. Deploy and tune a web application firewall so it blocks abuse without blocking checkout.
