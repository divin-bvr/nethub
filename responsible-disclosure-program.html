Security

Responsible disclosure

If you find a weakness in a Nethub site, lab host, or service we run, tell us privately. Do not use lab skills against systems you do not have permission to test.

What to send

A short description, how to reproduce, and whether student data or a client network could be affected. Use the contact page.

What we ask

Give us a reasonable window to fix before public write-ups. Do not extract more data than needed to prove the issue. This is not a paid bug bounty unless we say so in writing.

We learn it. We apply it. We report it.
